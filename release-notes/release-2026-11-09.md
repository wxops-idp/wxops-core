## Highlights

The guardrails of `auth.wxops.cloud` now **fail closed by default**: every validating `XPlatformPolicy` runs in `Deny` with `failurePolicy: Fail`, the
third layer after login and RBAC. Around it, the policy layer gets what teams kept asking for:

- **owner mapping** — a second team stands for an owner through one `wxops.cloud/owner` label on its `XScmTeam`, read by personas, bindings and every policy
  alike ([ADR-006](../development-docs/adr/006-team-owner-mapping.md));
- **scope per template** (`namespaceSelector`);
- **an environment gate** (`env-gate`, optional) that keeps a `prod` namespace changed through Git, or by the persona trusted with it;
- **a time-bound exemption** (`temporaryExemptPersonaRef`) beside the standing one.

Two misleading names are gone: the `secret-readonly` template is `secret-guard`, and the built-in `view` is no longer bindable. Proven on a cluster in the
sandbox; `./run.sh mapping` and `./run.sh env-gate` replay the new pieces.

What those rows mean:

- **`platform-policy`**
  - **`breaking`:** the template `secret-readonly` is renamed `secret-guard`. Same rule — people do not write Secrets, External Secrets Operator does,
    from OpenBao — under a name that no longer suggests people may read them. The `replay-invalid` rows are the three released test cases that still
    carried the old name.
  - **`careful`:**
    - `mode` defaults to `Deny` (was `Audit`); the `content-changed` rows are the cases that left `mode` out, now `[Deny]`/`Fail`;
    - `owner-stamp` also runs on UPDATE, labelling an object that predates the policy on its next update;
    - `namespace-guard` also guards `wxops.cloud/environment`;
    - `env-gate` joins the template list.
  - **`safe`:** `namespaceSelector`, `temporaryExemptPersonaRef`, `environments`, and owner mapping in the `teams` variable.
- **`persona-binding` · `breaking`:** `role: view` is removed. `wxops:persona-viewers` is `view` plus the pieces the platform adds for viewers — bind that.
  `scope: owned` now also binds a team in the namespaces of the owner it is mapped to.
- **`persona` · `safe`:** a new status field, `status.owners`.

**Rolling back** to `release-2026-11-04` reinstalls its XRDs: the new fields (`namespaceSelector`, `temporaryExemptPersonaRef`, `environments`) are pruned
from XRs that use them, `env-gate` and `secret-guard` policies stop validating, and the default returns to `Audit` — undo the migration below first.

## Upgrade notes

- **Before upgrading, move off the two removed names** — an XR still using one stops validating:

  ```bash
  # template secret-readonly → secret-guard. The Kyverno policy is named after the XR, so enforcement is not interrupted.
  kubectl get xplatformpolicies -o jsonpath='{range .items[*]}{.metadata.name} {.spec.parameters.template}{"\n"}{end}' | grep -w secret-readonly
  # role view → wxops:persona-viewers
  kubectl get xpersonabindings -o jsonpath='{range .items[*]}{.metadata.name} {.spec.parameters.role}{"\n"}{end}' | grep -w view
  ```

- **`Deny` is now the default.** A policy that left `mode` out starts refusing the moment the new XRD is applied — the API server applies the default on
  read. List them first: `kubectl get xplatformpolicies -o custom-columns=NAME:.metadata.name,MODE:.spec.parameters.mode`. `mode: Audit` keeps the old
  behaviour but is **not supported**: it records only into PolicyReports, which W'xOps does not run.
- **Run Kyverno in high availability** — at least three admission-controller replicas and a PodDisruptionBudget, per
  [Kyverno's guide](https://kyverno.io/docs/guides/high-availability/): under `Fail`, what Kyverno cannot check is refused. An optional Kyverno-required
  guard is described in [setup](../docs/user-guide/setup.md#optional-kyverno-required-guard--before-any-platform-policy).
- **Re-apply the provider RBAC** — `make providers-rbac`: provider-kubernetes no longer holds `bind` on `view`.
- **Owner mapping:** label an `XScmTeam` `wxops.cloud/owner: <owner>` — only the platform team writes `XScmTeam`s. It reaches personas, bindings and
  policies at their next reconcile; nudge a policy to see it sooner: `kubectl annotate xplatformpolicy <name> wxops.test/nudge="$(date +%s)" --overwrite`.
- **Temporary exemption:** `temporaryExemptPersonaRef: {name, until}`, `until` in UTC with `Z` — `date -u -d '+2 hours' +%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ` (macOS:
  `date -u -v+2H …`). Add it to the policy that refuses, never as a second policy: every matching policy must allow a request.
- **`env-gate`:** label the namespaces to guard `wxops.cloud/environment: <env>` (the platform team's; `namespace-guard` keeps it so) and give the trusted
  persona `environments` including it.
- **Namespace defaults** — quota, limits, network policy and registry credentials — are bootstrapped from GitOps, not Core
  ([access model](../development-docs/core-ideas/access-model.md#namespace-defaults-come-from-gitops--decided-2026-11-08)).

## Known issues

- **An exemption only gets past a policy; RBAC decides first.** Exempting a team persona from `secret-guard` does nothing: team roles cannot write Secrets.
- **Changes converge, they do not switch** — a new owner mapping or persona change reaches a policy at its next reconcile.
- **A namespace selector never skips a cluster-scoped object**: `owner-seal` on Core XRs and `rbac-guard`'s cluster bindings apply everywhere.
- **Tenants share one `tenants/` `ClusterSecretStore`**, so an `ExternalSecret` can name another team's OpenBao path; per-owner stores are planned with
  [RFC-008](../development-docs/rfc/008-built-in-tools-identity-and-catalog.md).
- **`environments` on `XPersona` is enforced per namespace by `env-gate`**, not yet per cluster — that waits for the multi-cluster cluster identity.
- Kyverno HA and the optional guard are the operator's setup and were not re-run against this release's cluster.
